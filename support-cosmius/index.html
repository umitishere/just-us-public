Gameplay and Progress

Cosmius saves local progress such as Nebulite balance, upgrades, selected language, quest progress, best run, and ad cooldown state on the device. If you reset progress inside the app or delete the app, local progress may be removed.

Rewarded Ads

Rewarded ads are optional. They can be used to double eligible quest rewards or continue once after a run ends. If an ad is on cooldown or unavailable, the app may show a wait timer before that rewarded option can be used again.
