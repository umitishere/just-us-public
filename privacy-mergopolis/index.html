Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy covers Mergopolis for iPhone, iPad and Android, developed by Ümit Sancaklı. You can play without signing in: the game creates an account for you automatically.

Your account and cloud save

On first launch Mergopolis creates an account for you (through Google Firebase) so your town can be saved in the cloud and used with friends. We store:

  • An account ID and a player number (for example #123456).
  • The username you choose, if you set one.
  • Your saved town and progress (buildings, merge board, energy, level and settings).
  • Your friends list, friend requests, gifts, players you block and reports you send.
  • Your level and points on the leaderboard.
  • On iPhone and iPad, if you choose Sign in with Apple: the email address Apple shares with us (it may be a private relay address).

This data is used only to run the game: saving and restoring your town, friends, gifts and the leaderboard. Your username, player number, level and published town are visible to other players (for example to friends you add and on the leaderboard). We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

Advertising & diagnostics

Mergopolis uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads. Watching a completed rewarded ad can grant in-game rewards. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process:

  • Device or app identifiers, including an advertising identifier when permitted by your device settings and permissions.
  • IP addresses and the approximate location inferred from them.
  • Ad impressions, ad interactions, and other app interaction data.
  • Crash reports, performance information, and other diagnostics.

This information may be used to deliver and measure ads, analyze usage, prevent fraud, and improve service reliability. Advertising data practices depend on your region, choices, device permissions, and the ad service configuration.

On iPhone and iPad, the Meta SDK counts app installs and app opens to measure our ads; the advertising identifier is not collected by it.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure for Android and iOS for more information. Test versions (TestFlight and Google Play testing) may use test ads, which still use Google’s advertising SDK.

Your choices

Rewarded ads and usernames are optional. You can manage advertising and tracking permissions in your device’s privacy settings. Where a consent message or advertising privacy options are available in the app, you can use them to review your choices. Device permissions alone do not prevent all diagnostic or advertising service data collection.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and investigate your request, and retain it only as needed for support and any applicable legal obligations. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information. GitHub issue reports are public.

Retention & deletion

Your account and cloud save are kept until you delete them. You can delete your account and all its data in the game at any time (Settings › Account › Delete account), or ask us by email; see Delete your Mergopolis account. Data handled by Google is retained according to its policies. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence or ask about your privacy rights.

Children’s privacy

Mergopolis is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The date above identifies the latest version.

Türkçe özet

Mergopolis giriş yapmadan oynanır; ilk açılışta sizin için otomatik bir hesap oluşturulur. Hesap kimliği, oyuncu numarası, seçtiğiniz kullanıcı adı, kasabanız ve ilerlemeniz, arkadaşlarınız, hediyeler ve skor tablosu bilgileri oyunu çalıştırmak için bulutta (Google Firebase) saklanır. Hesabınızı ve tüm verilerini oyunda Ayarlar › Hesap › Hesabı sil ile veya bize e-posta göndererek silebilirsiniz. İsteğe bağlı ödüllü reklamlar için Google AdMob kullanılır; reklam hizmetleri cihaz tanımlayıcıları, IP adresinden tahmin edilen yaklaşık konum, reklam etkileşimleri ve tanılama verilerini işleyebilir.
