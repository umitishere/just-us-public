Cosmius

Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Cosmius iOS game.

Overview

Cosmius is an arcade space shooter. The app does not require account sign-up and does not ask you to submit personal profile information. Game progress is stored locally on your device.

Data We Collect

Cosmius uses Google AdMob for advertising, including optional rewarded ads. Through AdMob and related advertising partners, the following data may be collected or processed:

  • Device identifiers, such as advertising identifiers, when permitted by your device settings.
  • Ad interaction data, app usage data, diagnostics, and crash-related information.
  • Approximate location inferred from your IP address, used for ad delivery, fraud prevention, analytics, and service reliability.

Local App Data

The app may store local gameplay data on your device, including selected language, selected episode, Nebulite balance, upgrade levels, quest progress, best run, rewarded ad cooldowns, daily rewarded ad counts, and settings preferences. This data is used to run the game and keep your progress between sessions.

Rewarded Ads

Rewarded ads are optional. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Cosmius may grant in-game rewards such as doubled quest rewards or one continue after a run ends. Reward availability may be limited by cooldowns and daily caps.

Third-Party Services

Cosmius uses Google AdMob for advertising. You can review Google's privacy policy here: policies.google.com/privacy.

Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you reset progress in the app, delete the app, or your device removes the data. Data processed by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's policies and your device privacy settings.

Children's Privacy

Cosmius is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact us at umitsancakli98@gmail.com. You can also open a public issue here: GitHub Issues.
