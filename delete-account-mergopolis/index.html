Delete your account

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Mergopolis by Ümit Sancaklı creates an account for you automatically. You can delete it, with all its data, at any time.

In the game

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and can’t be undone. The game then starts again with a new, empty account.

By email

If you no longer have the game, email umitsancakli98@gmail.com with the subject “Mergopolis account deletion” and your player number (for example #123456) or username. We delete the account and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account ID, player number and username.
  • Your saved town and progress.
  • Your friends, friend requests, gifts, blocked players and leaderboard entry.
  • The email address linked through Sign in with Apple (iPhone and iPad), if any.

Reports you sent about other players may be kept to handle abuse. Advertising data handled by Google is retained according to Google’s policies.
