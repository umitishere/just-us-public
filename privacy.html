Just Us

Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Just Us iOS app.

Overview

Just Us is a questions and recommendation app for couples. The app does not require account sign-up.

Data We Collect

Just Us shows ads using Google AdMob. Through AdMob and related advertising partners, the following data may be collected and processed for ad delivery, fraud prevention, performance measurement, and diagnostics:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID/IDFA, if permission is granted)
  • App interaction and usage data
  • Device and performance diagnostics (for crashes, latency, and reliability)
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address

Depending on your settings and consent choices, ad data may be used to show personalized or non-personalized ads.

Local App Data

The app may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Selected app language
  • Favorite question IDs
  • Saved recommendation idea IDs
  • Basic local app preferences

Purchases

Premium purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive or store payment card details.

Third-Party Services

Just Us uses Google AdMob for advertising. You can review Google policies here: policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

Just Us is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
