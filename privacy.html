Just Us

Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Just Us iOS app.

Overview

Just Us is an offline-first couple questions and recommendation app. The app does not require an account and does not use a backend server.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data through the Just Us app.

Local App Data

The app may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Selected app language
  • Favorite question IDs
  • Saved recommendation idea IDs
  • Basic local app preferences

This information stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Purchases

Premium purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Just Us uses Apple's StoreKit framework to check purchase entitlement. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Third-Party Services

Just Us does not use Firebase, CloudKit, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or AI APIs.

Children's Privacy

Just Us is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, please open a public issue on GitHub: github.com/umitishere/just-us-public/issues
